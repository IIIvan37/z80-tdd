## CPC timing data

The NOP count of every instruction (`src/nops.ts`) comes from "Perfectly accurate Z80 flags and CPC timing", v1.0.6, by Madram / Overlanders, measured on a real Amstrad CPC: <https://64nops.wordpress.com/2021/01/13/perfectly-accurate-z80-flags-and-cpc-timing/>. The source asks that anything derived from it credit Madram / Overlanders.
